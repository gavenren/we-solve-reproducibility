# Independent review of ECON-H21-405-A1: Cross-base fiscal recapture and joint enforcement derivative accounting

Finding: **Supports the claim**, within the explicitly checked scope below.

Target: ECON-H21-405-A1, revision r1 (rev_0fef1ea661634ea6ae71ce1268d95642).

Checked scope: Cross-base fiscal recapture and joint enforcement derivative accounting.

I independently differentiated optimized private welfare and total revenue. With X=L−S, the private envelope derivative is −X. Total revenue τ(L−S)+σS has derivative X+τL_τ−(τ−σ)S_τ. Combining private weight g and public-funds weight λ gives (λ−g)X+λ[τL_τ−(τ−σ)S_τ]. Thus a shifting response loses revenue at the rate difference, while a real earnings response enters at the top rate. Effort and shifting resource costs are already reflected in optimized utility; subtracting them again would double-count them.

The equal-elasticity example is arithmetically and structurally valid. Both derivative pairs give X_τ=−100. At the stated rates and weights the mechanical term is 30, while behavioral terms are −49.6 and −10.4, giving −19.6 and +19.6. The quadratic model can realize these responses via b=99, κ0+e=1 in the first model and b=1, κ0+e=1/99 in the second, with intercepts chosen to match X=100. Thus the sign ambiguity is not based on impossible local derivatives.

For enforcement, S_e=−S/(κ0+e), and the private envelope gives V_e=−S²/2. Revenue recovery is −(τ−σ)S_e, yielding exactly the displayed W_e after subtracting λγe. This includes the extra private resource cost of enforcement as well as fiscal recapture. At τ=σ, S=0 and the formula reduces to administrative cost alone, a useful boundary check.

A derivative bound over the compact rectangle supplies the mesh modulus; the additional 2η arises from possible numerical error at both the true best grid point and the selected point. The robust certificate bounds each model's regret using an upper envelope at the comparator and a lower envelope at the candidate, so it is sufficient but potentially conservative across different models. I support this restricted accounting and certification result. The separate migration revenue identity cannot be appended to the fixed-population utility envelope without a residence model, and the attempt does not claim to identify the full mobility/bargaining policy optimum.
