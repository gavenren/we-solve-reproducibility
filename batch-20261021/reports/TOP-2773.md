# Injective irreducible surface monodromies admitting sections through a boundary lift

**Problem:** TOP-2773

**Claim type:** Literature report

**Exact scope:** Baykur–Margalit's explicit injective irreducible monodromies for every fiber genus g≥2 and base genus h≥2 lift to the one-boundary mapping class group, and therefore their bundles have an unbranched degree-one multisection with trivial normal bundle. The lifting-to-section deduction is supplied. Arbitrary injective monodromy is not covered.

There are nontrivial affirmative examples satisfying the injectivity hypothesis in every pair of genera \(g,h\ge2\). Baykur–Margalit construct injective irreducible homomorphisms
\[
 \rho:\pi_1(\Sigma_h)\hookrightarrow\operatorname{Mod}(\Sigma_g).
\]
Their construction factors, as an actual homomorphism, through
\[
 \pi_1(\Sigma_h)\longrightarrow A
 \longrightarrow B_{2g+1}
 \longrightarrow\operatorname{Mod}(\Sigma_g^1)
 \longrightarrow\operatorname{Mod}(\Sigma_g),
\]
where \(\Sigma_g^1\) has one boundary circle fixed pointwise. The final arrow caps that boundary by a disk. The source proves injectivity of the composite despite the kernel of the final capping map, and proves irreducibility of its image. These are deep construction results credited to the authors.

We explain why the boundary lift gives more than a branched multisection. Present the base as one vertex, \(2h\) edges, and a two-cell with attaching word \(\prod_{i=1}^h[a_i,b_i]\). Choose boundary-fixing diffeomorphism representatives of the lifted mapping classes for the edges. Since the lifted classes satisfy the relation, the corresponding product is isotopic to the identity through boundary-fixing diffeomorphisms. Use that isotopy to extend the fiber bundle over the two-cell. Representatives and isotopy may be chosen equal to the identity on a collar of the boundary. Cap the fiber boundary by a disk, and extend all transition maps and the relation isotopy by the identity on this disk.

The cap disks consequently form a product disk subbundle \(\Sigma_h\times D^2\). Their centers form an embedded smooth section \(C\), with \(p|_C\) the identity on \(\Sigma_h\). In particular this is an unbranched degree-one multisection: there are no branch points, collisions or singular fibers. The product disk neighborhood also shows its normal Euler number is zero.

For \(g\ge2\), a surface bundle over a surface is determined up to bundle equivalence by its mapping-class monodromy, so this capped bundle realizes the specified \(\rho\). Equivalently, the construction itself supplies the surface bundle associated with that lift. Thus one obtains bundles with injective irreducible monodromy and an honest section, rather than merely bundles with reducible or trivial monodromy.

Irreducibility of a subgroup of the mapping class group says it preserves no essential multicurve. It does **not** forbid a lift fixing a disk: that disk is inessential after capping. Likewise the source's “section-sum indecomposable” property prohibits a particular decomposition along sections, not the existence of a section itself.

This family answers an existence subquestion with all genera represented. The literal universal statement is stronger: it asks for some finite-degree multisection for every injective monodromy, even one with no boundary lift. No such lift or finite configuration is constructed for an arbitrary bundle here.
