# Zero torsion under strong fillability and unbounded torsion across tight tori

**Problem:** TOP-2840

**Claim type:** Literature report

**Exact scope:** Every strongly symplectically fillable contact 3-manifold has Giroux torsion zero. On the fixed manifold T³, tight structures realize arbitrarily large lower bounds on Giroux torsion. This separates pointwise finiteness from a false uniform bound; it does not establish finiteness for an arbitrary fixed tight structure.

There are two quite different quantifiers in this question. Gay's Corollary 2.2 proves that an embedded full-turn Giroux torsion layer obstructs a **strong** symplectic filling. Therefore
\[
 (Y,\xi)\text{ strongly fillable}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 \operatorname{GT}(Y,\xi)=0.                                      \tag{1}
\]
The source uses a \(\pi t\) angular parameter on a layer of length \(2n\), which is precisely \(n\) full \(2\pi\) turns in the prompt's normalization. Thus even a single positive integral layer is excluded, regardless of its embedding.

The obstruction is geometric: Gay constructs a local symplectic cobordism carrying chains of square-zero symplectic spheres. Gluing it to a supposed strong filling and applying the credited gauge-theoretic obstruction gives a contradiction. A boundary Liouville field is used in the argument. Weak fillability has no such boundary hypothesis and is not excluded by this theorem.

In the other direction, no uniform bound is possible even while keeping the underlying manifold fixed. On \(T^3=\mathbb R^3/\mathbb Z^3\), put
\[
 \xi_n=\ker(\cos(2\pi nz)\,dx+\sin(2\pi nz)\,dy),\qquad n\geq1 .
\]
These structures are tight by the checked Kanda classification source. Choose the contact orientation determined by the displayed form. For any positive integer \(N<n\), define
\[
 e_N:T^2\times[0,1]\longrightarrow T^3,\qquad
 e_N(x,y,t)=(x,y,Nt/n)\pmod{\mathbb Z^3}.
\]
Since \(0\leq Nt/n\leq N/n<1\), this is an embedding of the entire closed layer, including its distinct boundary tori. Pulling back the defining form gives exactly
\[
 \cos(2\pi Nt)\,dx+\sin(2\pi Nt)\,dy.
\]
Consequently \(\operatorname{GT}(T^3,\xi_n)\geq n-1\). Given any proposed bound depending only on the topology of \(T^3\), taking \(n\) large contradicts it.

This is not a counterexample to the prompt: the contact structure changes with \(n\). The desired finiteness statement allows its bound to depend on the entire pair \((Y,\xi)\). Nor does the explicit lower-bound embedding prove an upper bound for all other embeddings into a fixed \(\xi_n\). Equation (1) settles the strongly fillable scope, while the tight torus family explains why no topological uniform bound or weak-fillability shortcut can replace the actual pointwise question.
