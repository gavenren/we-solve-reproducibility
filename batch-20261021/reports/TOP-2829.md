# A terminating left-orderability decision for graph-manifold rational homology spheres

**Problem:** TOP-2829

**Claim type:** Literature report

**Exact scope:** On the promised class of closed connected orientable irreducible graph-manifold rational homology spheres with nontrivial fundamental group, left-orderability is decidable by exact mod-two hat Heegaard Floer rank. The report specifies termination and finite trace certificates measured in their expanded size, without asserting a polynomial bound in triangulation size or a decision procedure for all three-manifolds.

Let \(Y\) belong to the promised class in the scope. The HRRW Theorem 2, completing Boyer–Clay's results, gives
\[
 \pi_1Y\text{ left-orderable}
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 Y\text{ is not an L-space}.
\]
With the source's coefficient field \(\mathbb F_2\), put
\(r=\dim_{\mathbb F_2}\widehat{HF}(Y;\mathbb F_2)\) and
\(h=|H_1(Y;\mathbb Z)|\). The criterion is \(r>h\); equality is the L-space case.

This turns into a terminating decision on this whole JSJ class, not just on single Seifert pieces. From a finite triangulation construct a Heegaard diagram by taking regular neighborhoods of the dual skeleta. Make it pointed and admissible by the usual finite diagram operations. Sarkar–Wang's Theorem 1.2 gives a terminating isotopy/handleslide conversion to a nice diagram, and Theorem 1.1 makes its differential combinatorial. In a nice diagram the relevant disks are empty embedded bigons and squares. List the finitely many generators, form the differential matrix \(D\) over \(\mathbb F_2\), and compute
\[
 r=N-2\operatorname{rank}(D)
\]
for the total chain dimension \(N\). Compute \(h\) from integral homology by Smith normal form. Compare the two integers and output the corresponding answer. The deep equivalence and combinatorial Floer theorem are credited imported inputs.

There are finite certificates for both answers in the following explicit, modest sense. Retain the Heegaard-diagram construction and admissibility operations, each isotopy/handleslide in the nice-diagram conversion, the resulting regions, the complete expanded enumeration of every candidate tuple of intersection points (including every rejected non-generator and its rejection witness), the generator list, the full differential matrix, and row-reduction records for that matrix and for homology. A verifier can check every local diagram operation, enumerate the bigons/squares and verify exact elimination. These checks are polynomial in a fully expanded encoding of the trace, complete candidate enumeration, generator list and matrices: checking a move is local combinatorics, and matrix elimination is polynomial in matrix dimension and entry bit size.

This statement does not bound that expanded trace or \(N\) polynomially in the input tetrahedra. Diagram conversion can greatly enlarge intersections, and generator counts can grow combinatorially. Thus it is a finite verifiable-certificate claim in terms of certificate length, not membership in NP or coNP for the triangulation encoding. The graph-manifold restriction is a promise here; this report does not claim a short certificate recognizing that promise.

The requirement that the group be nontrivial is explicit because the prompt excludes the trivial group from left-orderability. The general hyperbolic rational-homology-sphere decision and general complexity bounds are not inferred from the proven graph-manifold equivalence.
