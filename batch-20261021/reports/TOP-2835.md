# Integral linearity for mixed three-manifold groups, including finite-extension faithfulness

**Problem:** TOP-2835

**Claim type:** Literature report

**Exact scope:** Every compact connected oriented irreducible mixed three-manifold, with arbitrary possibly empty boundary, has fundamental group linear over Z by Przytycki–Wise. Given an index-m subgroup with a faithful d-dimensional integral representation, the report constructs a faithful md-dimensional representation of the full group. It gives no uniform or triangulation-effective bound on m or d and no claim for all closed graph-manifold groups.

Call a compact connected oriented irreducible three-manifold mixed when it is neither hyperbolic nor a graph manifold, in the terminology of Przytycki–Wise. Their Theorem 1.1 proves that its fundamental group \(G\) is virtually special, and their Corollary 1.2 proves that \(G\) is linear over \(\mathbb Z\). This covers manifolds assembled from hyperbolic and Seifert pieces, not merely one individual geometric piece. It is therefore a substantial positive regime for part (a).

The finite-extension step deserves an explicit proof, since a representation of a finite cover alone would not yet establish faithfulness for \(G\). Suppose \(H\le G\) has index \(m\) and \(\rho:H\hookrightarrow\mathrm{GL}_d(\mathbb Z)\) is faithful. Choose left coset representatives \(t_1=1,t_2,\ldots,t_m\), so \(G=\bigsqcup t_iH\). Let
\[
 W=\mathbb Z[G]\otimes_{\mathbb Z[H]}\mathbb Z^d
    =\bigoplus_{i=1}^m t_i\otimes\mathbb Z^d.
\]
Here \(\mathbb Z[G]\) is a right \(\mathbb Z[H]\)-module and \(H\) acts on \(\mathbb Z^d\) through \(\rho\). Left multiplication by \(G\) defines an integral representation of dimension \(md\). If \(g t_i=t_j h\), its \(i\)-th summand is sent to the \(j\)-th by the matrix \(\rho(h)\). Thus each matrix is a permutation of invertible integral blocks, and products follow associativity of multiplication in \(G\).

If \(g\) acts as the identity, apply it to the first summand. Equality \(g(1\otimes v)=1\otimes v\) for every \(v\) first forces \(gH=H\), hence \(g\in H\). On that same summand the action is \(\rho(g)\), so faithfulness gives \(g=1\). This proves the representation of the **whole** group is faithful, even when \(H\) is not normal.

For the mixed-manifold application, the source's special-subgroup theorem and integral linearity of finite-generator right-angled Artin groups provide such \(H,\rho\). The displayed construction then yields
\[
 d_0(G)\le md .
\]
It is a conditional numerical bound in terms of actual finite-index and representation data; it is not an algorithmic bound derived from the number of input tetrahedra. The deep theorem supplies existence, and this report does not certify an effective procedure or uniform maximum dimension.

Finally, irreducibility, compactness, and the exclusion of graph manifolds are retained. The result cannot be applied to every finitely generated three-manifold group merely by asserting that each piece separately has some representation; compatibility across gluing is precisely what virtual specialness supplies in this class.
