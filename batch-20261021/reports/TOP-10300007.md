# A full parameterization of metrics making a fixed smooth foliation minimal

**Problem:** TOP-10300007

**Claim type:** Partial result

**Exact scope:** For a smooth cooriented taut foliation of a closed connected oriented 3-manifold, all metrics making the fixed foliation minimal are explicitly parameterized and form a contractible space. Metrics allowing an isotopy are exactly its Diff₀ orbit; the topology of that orbit is not claimed to be classified.

Assume \(M\) is closed connected oriented and \(\mathcal F\) is a smooth cooriented taut foliation. Fix a positive defining one-form \(\beta\), so \(T\mathcal F=\ker\beta\). Define the convex cone
\[
 \mathcal Z_{\mathcal F}=\{\eta\in\Omega^2(M):d\eta=0,\
                    \eta|_{T\mathcal F}>0\}.
\]
The Rummler–Sullivan construction, as explicitly proved in the checked reference, makes this cone nonempty.

Here is a parameterization of **every** metric for which \(\mathcal F\) itself is minimal. Choose:
\[
 \eta\in\mathcal Z_{\mathcal F},\quad u\in C^\infty(M,\mathbb R_{>0}),
 \quad q\text{ a metric on }T\mathcal F
       \text{ with area form }\eta|_{T\mathcal F}.
\]
The kernel of \(\eta\) is a line transverse to \(T\mathcal F\). Let \(T\) be its unique section with \(\beta(T)=1\). Declare
\[
 TM=T\mathcal F\oplus\mathbb RT,\qquad
 g=q\oplus(u\beta)^2.                                             \tag{1}
\]
Thus the two summands are orthogonal and \(g(T,T)=u^2\).

The positive unit normal is \(N=T/u\), and
\[
 \operatorname{vol}_g=u\beta\wedge\eta,\qquad
 \iota_N\operatorname{vol}_g=\eta.
\]
Cartan's formula gives
\(d\eta=(\operatorname{div}_gN)\operatorname{vol}_g\).
For an orthonormal leaf frame \(e_1,e_2\), the unit-length condition gives
\(\langle\nabla_NN,N\rangle=0\), so
\(\operatorname{div}N=\sum_i\langle\nabla_{e_i}N,e_i\rangle\).
Up to the immaterial choice of mean-curvature sign this is the trace of the second fundamental form. Hence (1) makes every leaf minimal.

Conversely, start with any such minimal metric. Its positive unit normal defines \(\eta=\iota_N\operatorname{vol}_g\). The same divergence computation shows \(d\eta=0\); it is positive on the leaves and has kernel \(\mathbb RN\). Put \(u=1/\beta(N)\), \(T=uN\), and \(q=g|_{T\mathcal F}\). These data reconstruct the metric by (1), uniquely. Thus no minimal metrics have been omitted.

The parameter space is contractible in the smooth topology. To see this without relying merely on contractible fibers, fix a leaf metric \(q_0\) with area form \(\omega_0\), and write \(\eta|_{T\mathcal F}=f_\eta\omega_0\). Every permitted \(q\) is uniquely
\[
 q(v,w)=f_\eta\,q_0(e^A v,w),
\]
where \(A\) is a smooth \(q_0\)-self-adjoint traceless endomorphism of \(T\mathcal F\). The positive matrix logarithm supplies the inverse, since determinant one is equivalent to trace-zero logarithm. Consequently the space is parameterized by the nonempty convex cone \(\mathcal Z_{\mathcal F}\), the convex cone of positive functions, and a vector space of sections \(A\). Linear contraction in these coordinates proves the claim.

Finally the prompt allows an ambient isotopy. Naturality gives
\[
 h_*\mathcal F\text{ minimal for }g
 \iff \mathcal F\text{ minimal for }h^*g.
\]
The exact requested set, on this smooth/cooriented/closed scope, is therefore
\(\bigcup_{h\in\operatorname{Diff}_0(M)}(h^{-1})^*\mathcal G_{\mathcal F}\),
with \(\mathcal G_{\mathcal F}\) explicitly described above. This is an exact orbit description, not a claim that the orbit is contractible or that deciding orbit membership is automatic.
