# Odd metacyclic defect blocks: a uniform bound and a sharp nonabelian ratio

Claim type: **Literature report**.

We add an infinite **defect-group** class to the earlier elementary ambient-group cases: for an odd prime$p$, Brauer's bound holds for every block of any finite group whose defect group$D$is metacyclic. Here metacyclic means a cyclic normal subgroup with cyclic quotient, and the ambient group need not be soluble. This is a credited theorem, not a deduction from arbitrary Gram-matrix data.

The checked source is Sambale[1, Section8.2, Corollary8.11], with$p>2$as that section's convention. It proves$k(B)\le|D|$for all such defect groups. For the nonabelian split case its Proposition8.9 gives the stronger height-weighted information
\[
 \sum_{i\ge0}p^{2i}k_i(B)\le|D|,
 \qquad k_0(B)\le|D:D'|, \tag{1}
\]
where$k_i(B)$counts ordinary irreducible characters in the block of height$i$. These structural block inputs are imported explicitly. In particular$k_0$counts height-zero characters, not modular simple modules$\ell(B)$.

Here is an immediate quantitative consequence that records how far from equality these blocks must lie. Put$H=\sum_{i\ge1}k_i(B)$. From(1),
\[
 k_0(B)+p^2H\le|D|,
\]
so
\[
 k(B)=k_0(B)+H
 \le\frac{|D|}{p^2}+\left(1-\frac1{p^2}\right)k_0(B)
 \le\frac{|D|}{p^2}+\left(1-\frac1{p^2}\right)|D:D'|. \tag{2}
\]
If$D$is nonabelian, $|D'|\ge p$, giving the uniform strict bound
\[
 \frac{k(B)}{|D|}\le\frac{p^2+p-1}{p^3}<1. \tag{3}
\]
Thus(2)uses genuine height information to improve the target; merely replacing a Cartan determinant by its largest elementary divisor would not yield it. The report does not assert(1)for arbitrary nonmetacyclic defect groups.

The coefficient in(3)is sharp for this class. For any odd$p$, consider
\[
 P=\langle x,y:x^{p^2}=y^p=1,\ yxy^{-1}=x^{1+p}\rangle.
\]
The action has order$p$, so this is a split metacyclic group of order$p^3$. Its commutator subgroup is$\langle x^p\rangle$of order$p$, and its center is the same subgroup: commuting with$x$forces the exponent of$y$to be0, and commuting with$y$forces the exponent of$x$to be divisible by$p$. Every noncentral conjugacy class has size$p$. For a noncentral$x^ay^b$, if$b=0$then$\langle x\rangle$lies in its centralizer of order at least$p^2$; if$b\ne0$, the subgroup generated by it and the central subgroup has order at least$p^2$. The centralizer is proper, so its order is exactly$p^2$. Hence
\[
 k(P)=|Z(P)|+\frac{|P|-|Z(P)|}{p}=p^2+p-1.
\]
In characteristic$p$the group algebra of a$p$-group is local, so its unique block has defect$P$and all its ordinary characters. Thus$k(B)=k(P)$and equality holds in(3). This proves sharpness uniformly in every odd prime, rather than checking one character table.

The earlier A1 treated$p$-groups, abelian groups, direct products with$p'$-groups, $\ell(B)=1$, and general quadratic-form certificates. It did not establish the theorem for **all ambient groups with an odd metacyclic defect**, nor the height-weighted ratio(2)and its sharp family. The elementary$p$-group example here is used only to certify sharpness of that stronger all-ambient bound. The new structural theorem is credited to the source, not claimed novel.

The nonsplit metacyclic case is included in the source's Brauer-bound conclusion; the quantitative argument(1)–(3)is stated only for the nonabelian **split**case where the inspected weighted estimate applies. No equality classification for arbitrary blocks or extension to all defect groups is claimed.
