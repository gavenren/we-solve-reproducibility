# P=W on the full torsion-invariant summand in every composite rank

Claim type: **Literature report**.

For **every composite rank**$r\ge4$, every$g\ge2$ and degree$d$ coprime to$r$, the equality requested in the prompt holds on the full invariant cohomology under
\[
 \Gamma=\operatorname{Pic}^0(C)[r]\cong(\mathbb Z/r\mathbb Z)^{2g}:
\qquad
 P_kH^m(X,\mathbb Q)^\Gamma
 =W_{2k}H^m(X^B,\mathbb Q)^\Gamma
 =W_{2k+1}H^m(X^B,\mathbb Q)^\Gamma. \tag{1}
\]
The remaining part is the variant cohomology, the kernel of averaging. This is a proper summand restriction on exactly the moduli spaces in the literal prompt, not a rank-two substitution for its composite-rank condition.

The two published source inputs fit together precisely. Maulik–Shen [1, Theorem0.2] prove$P=W$ for twisted$\mathrm{GL}_r$ moduli in all ranks, all genera$g\ge2$ and all coprime degrees. De Cataldo–Maulik–Shen [2, Proposition1.2] prove that this$\mathrm{GL}_r$ equality implies the equality for the$\Gamma$-invariant part of the fixed-determinant trace-free$\mathrm{SL}_r$ moduli, for the same curve/rank/degree. That proposition does **not** assume$r$ prime. Its later prime-rank variant-cohomology theorem is separate, so it is not used to claim the full composite-rank result.

Let us describe why invariants are the correct object. Tensoring a Higgs bundle$(E,\theta)$ by an$r$-torsion line bundle preserves$\det E=L$ and$\operatorname{tr}\theta=0$. It acts trivially on the Hitchin base and hence preserves the perverse filtration. On the Betti side the corresponding torsion rank-one local systems act by algebraic automorphisms, preserving the mixed Hodge structure and its weight filtration. The nonabelian Hodge correspondence matches the two actions. Rational averaging
\[
 e_\Gamma=\frac1{|\Gamma|}\sum_{\gamma\in\Gamma}\gamma
\]
is an idempotent on each cohomology group and on the filtered objects defining these filtrations. Therefore
\[
 H^m=H^m{}^\Gamma\oplus H^m_{\rm var},\qquad
 H^m_{\rm var}=\ker e_\Gamma,
\]
and both filtrations split compatibly. No freeness of the$\Gamma$ action on all moduli points is needed to define this averaging projector.

The quotient is the$\mathrm{PGL}_r$ moduli space/stack. Source [2, equations(8)–(9)] identifies its rational cohomology with$H^*(X)^\Gamma$, compatibly with the Hitchin perverse filtration on the Dolbeault side and with mixed Hodge structure on the Betti side. Passing from$\mathrm{GL}_r$ to$\mathrm{PGL}_r$ removes the rank-one determinant/trace factor; the finite determinant-root cover and Künneth comparison are exactly the reduction established by Proposition1.2. We invoke that checked filtered reduction rather than assuming that an unfiltered tensor decomposition by itself proves a statement about perverse filtrations.

Combining the two theorem statements gives$P_k=W_{2k}$ on invariants. The equality with$W_{2k+1}$ follows from the same source$\mathrm{GL}_r$ theorem and its weight-compatible quotient reduction: the relevant weight jumps there are even. In particular, taking$\Gamma$-invariants does not introduce new odd weight pieces. All filtration indices are those of the source normalization $P_k=W_{2k}$, matching the prompt; no dimension shift is silently suppressed.

Averaging does not establish anything on$\ker e_\Gamma$. An arbitrary class there averages to zero, so knowing(1) on every averaged class is compatible with any possible discrepancy of the two filtrations on that summand. The source's prime-rank theorem computes that variant part only under its prime hypothesis. Its discussion of composite-rank endoscopic pieces is not promoted here to a proved all-rank variant theorem. The new report therefore isolates exactly the substantial verified portion of the literal composite-rank question and exactly what these sources leave untreated.

Earlier A1 summarizes an unverified2026 theorem on full composite-rank cohomology. We have not checked that manuscript or a formalization, and do not use it. The present synthesis is based on published all-rank$\mathrm{GL}_r$ and invariant-reduction results, with the group action, summands and hypothesis boundaries made explicit.
