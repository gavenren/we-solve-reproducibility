# Infinite Reeb-orbit multiplicity with torsion Chern class, including degenerate forms

**Problem:** TOP-2844

**Claim type:** Literature report

**Exact scope:** For every closed connected non-lens 3-manifold, every contact form whose contact structure has torsion c₁ has infinitely many simple Reeb orbits, with no nondegeneracy assumption. In particular this covers all non-lens rational homology spheres. Forms with non-torsion c₁ are not included.

Let \(\lambda\) be a smooth contact form on a closed connected 3-manifold \(Y\), and orient \(\xi=\ker\lambda\) by \(d\lambda\). The checked theorem of Cristofaro-Gardiner–Hryniewicz–Hutchings–Liu is
\[
 c_1(\xi)\text{ torsion}
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 \lambda\text{ has either two or infinitely many simple Reeb orbits}.
                                                                  \tag{1}
\]
No nondegeneracy or genericity assumption is imposed. Corollary 1.7(a) eliminates the two-orbit case when \(Y\) is not a lens space, proving the requested infinitude on exactly this Chern-class scope.

The second input in that corollary is a classification theorem for a contact form with exactly two simple orbits: its underlying manifold is a lens space, both orbits are irrationally elliptic, and the contact structure is universally tight. Combining that classification with (1) yields the conclusion by contradiction. This is a credited deep theorem; the classification and holomorphic-curve estimates are not independently proved here.

One useful automatic range is every rational homology sphere. Its finitely generated group \(H^2(Y;\mathbb Z)\) has zero free rank by the universal coefficient theorem and rational Poincaré duality, hence is finite. Every \(c_1(\xi)\) is therefore torsion. Thus on any non-lens rational homology sphere the conclusion holds for *every* contact form, including arbitrarily degenerate ones. More generally it holds for each torsion-\(c_1\) contact structure on a non-lens manifold with positive first Betti number as well.

The theorem's proof uses embedded contact homology spectral asymptotics to construct Birkhoff-section information for perturbations and then controls the limiting holomorphic curves. These compactness and asymptotic inputs are why a mere perturbation argument would be insufficient: infinitely many periodic orbits on nearby nondegenerate flows need not persist as distinct simple orbits in a degenerate limit. The result quoted here supplies that missing control under its stated hypothesis.

Simple means a primitive periodic trajectory counted by its geometric image, not an iterate. Replacing each one orbit by all its multiple covers cannot satisfy the prompt. Finally, the source notes a separate result removing the Chern-class condition for nondegenerate forms; it does not remove that condition for arbitrary degenerate forms. This report does not claim the prompt for a degenerate form with non-torsion \(c_1\). The JAMS publication record is identified, while all theorem locators used here are explicitly those of the checked arXiv v2.
