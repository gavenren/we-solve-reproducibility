# A universal strict area bound for incompressible closed surfaces

**Problem:** TOP-2810

**Claim type:** Partial result

**Exact scope:** Every π1-injective smooth immersion of a closed connected orientable genus-g surface into a closed hyperbolic three-manifold has area strictly greater than 2π(g−1). This gives lower bounds for the closed-ambient immersed and embedded variants, including nonorientable ambients by an orientation-cover argument. No optimality or approaching sequence is claimed.

The lower bound proved here applies to the closed-ambient variants (a)–(c). It is not an answer giving their exact infima.

First suppose \(M\) is orientable. The Schoen–Yau minimizing theorem, in the precise exposition cited below, gives an area-minimizing smooth immersion \(h:S_g\to M\) in the homotopy class of a \(\pi_1\)-injective \(f\). Here \(M\) is compact and \(\pi_2(M)=0\). Thus \(A(h)\le A(f)\). The minimizer is two-sided because both \(S_g\) and \(M\) are orientable. Put \(B\) for its second fundamental form and \(A=A(h)\).

The Gauss equation and Gauss–Bonnet give
\[
 K=-1-\tfrac12|B|^2,\qquad
 4\pi(g-1)=A+\tfrac12\int_{S_g}|B|^2 .
\]
Stability under normal variations \(u\) gives
\[
 Q(u)=\int_{S_g}\bigl(|\nabla u|^2+(2-|B|^2)u^2\bigr)\ge0 .
\]
In particular \(u=1\) yields \(\int |B|^2\le2A\), so \(A\ge2\pi(g-1)\).

In fact equality is impossible. If equality held, \(Q(1)=0\). Apply nonnegativity to \(Q(1+tv)\), for every smooth \(v\) and both signs of \(t\). The linear term must vanish, hence
\[
 \int(2-|B|^2)v=0
\]
for all \(v\), and smoothness implies \(|B|^2=2\) everywhere. The principal curvatures of a minimal surface would therefore be the constants \(+1,-1\). Locally choose an orthonormal eigenframe \(e_1,e_2\). For the shape operator \(S e_1=e_1,\ S e_2=-e_2\), Codazzi says
\((\nabla_{e_1}S)e_2=(\nabla_{e_2}S)e_1\).
The left side is proportional to \(e_1\), the right to \(e_2\); their coefficients are twice the corresponding connection coefficients. Both vanish. The connection form is therefore zero on this neighborhood, forcing intrinsic \(K=0\). The Gauss equation instead gives \(K=-2\), a contradiction. Consequently
\[
 A(f)\ge A(h)>2\pi(g-1).
\]

If \(M\) is nonorientable, pull \(f\) back to the orientation double cover \(\widehat M\). A connected component \(\widehat S\) of this pullback covers \(S_g\) with degree \(d=1\) or \(2\), remains orientable and \(\pi_1\)-injective, and has genus \(\widehat g-1=d(g-1)\). Its area is \(dA(f)\). Applying the orientable case upstairs and dividing by \(d\) proves the same strict inequality.

Every embedding in the question is an immersion, so the bound also applies to embeddings without requiring an embedded minimizing representative. Taking infima gives at least \(2\pi(g-1)\) for fixed genus, and at least \(2\pi\) when genus varies. Strictness for each surface does **not** imply that the infimum is strictly larger: a nonattained limiting value is possible. Constructing an approaching family, improving this lower bound, and controlling the nonclosed-ambient variant are not accomplished here.
