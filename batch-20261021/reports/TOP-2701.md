# A corrected metabelian obstruction for a fixed realization, with the all-realizations quantifier retained

**Problem:** TOP-2701

**Claim type:** Literature report

**Exact scope:** Reconstructs the fixed-Seifert-form consequence of Kim's 2020 theorem in topological locally flat (hence smooth) concordance: for every metabolic M with nonunit Alexander polynomial and every V, some K realizing M⊕V is concordant to no J realizing V. Repairs the abelian-representation and polynomial-order steps in the printed proof. This is not an answer to the prompt's stronger quantifier over every realization of M⊕V.

Write \(\Lambda=\mathbb Q[t,t^{-1}]\), \(A_K=H_1(M_K;\Lambda)\), where \(M_K\) is zero surgery, and let \(\operatorname{Bl}_K\) denote the nonsingular rational Blanchfield pairing. Polynomials are understood up to Laurent units. Set
\[
 b(V)=\int_{S^1}\operatorname{sign}\big((1-\bar z)V+(1-z)V^T\big)\,dz ,
\]
with normalized circle measure. Values at the finitely many exceptional points do not affect this integral. For every knot with Seifert matrix \(V\), its abelian rho invariant is \(b(V)\), by COT's Proposition5.1. In particular it is fixed, even though the knot itself varies.

Kim's Lemma3.1 supplies the substantial construction input: for any nonunit-Alexander-polynomial Seifert form \(W\) and any prescribed \(C>0\), there is a knot \(K\) with form \(W\) such that
\[
 \rho(K,\phi_x)>C\qquad(0\ne x\in A_K).
\]
This is a uniform statement over all nonzero module elements. Its proof infects along an unlink off a Seifert surface using companions with large positive signature average. We import that construction; it is not an elementary claim or a new result here.

We reconstruct the fixed-form consequence with two proof details made explicit. Take \(W=M\oplus V\), where \(M\) is metabolic and \(\Delta_M\) is nonunit, and use the lemma with
\[
 C>|b(V)| .
\]
Because \(\Delta_W=\Delta_M\Delta_V\), it is nonunit. Choose the resulting \(K\). Suppose a knot \(J\) with form \(V\) were topologically locally flat concordant to \(K\). Then \(L=K\#(-J)\) is topologically slice. The COT metabelian slice obstruction gives a self-annihilating submodule
\[
 P=P^\perp\subset A_L=A_K\oplus A_{-J}
\]
for which \(\rho(L,\phi_x)=0\) for every \(x\in P\). COT works with locally flat topological slicing; thus smooth concordance is also excluded when the contradiction is obtained.

Here is a dimension proof that \(P\) contains \((a,0)\) with \(a\ne0\). For a finite torsion \(\Lambda\)-module \(T\), its rational dimension is the breadth of its order polynomial: for a cyclic summand multiply its annihilator by a Laurent unit to give nonzero constant term, and use the usual polynomial remainder basis. Dimensions add over the cyclic decomposition. Moreover
\[
 \dim_{\mathbb Q}\operatorname{Hom}_\Lambda
       (P,\mathbb Q(t)/\Lambda)=\dim_{\mathbb Q}P .
\]
This follows summand by summand, since elements killed by a nonzero polynomial \(p\) in the target are \(p^{-1}\Lambda/\Lambda\), isomorphic to \(\Lambda/(p)\). Conjugating the module by \(t\mapsto t^{-1}\) leaves this dimension unchanged.

Nonsingularity and restriction identify
\[
 T/P^\perp\ \cong\
 \overline{\operatorname{Hom}_\Lambda(P,\mathbb Q(t)/\Lambda)}.
\]
Restriction is onto: \(\mathbb Q(t)/\Lambda\) is divisible, hence injective over the PID \(\Lambda\), so each homomorphism from the submodule extends to \(T\). Therefore self-annihilation implies \(\dim_{\mathbb Q}P=\frac12\dim_{\mathbb Q}T\). Put \(m=\operatorname{breadth}\Delta_M>0\) and \(v=\operatorname{breadth}\Delta_V\). In our case
\[
 \dim_{\mathbb Q}P=\tfrac12(m+2v)>v=\dim_{\mathbb Q}A_{-J}.
\]
The projection \(P\to A_{-J}\) has nonzero kernel, proving the claim. This avoids the stronger, generally unjustified assertion that the order of a Blanchfield self-annihilator equals a literal polynomial square root of the whole order; in general an order is paired with its reciprocal conjugate.

The representation convention matters. For meridian \(\mu\) and abelianization \(\epsilon\), it is
\[
 \phi_x(y)=
 \big(\operatorname{Bl}(x,[y\mu^{-\epsilon(y)}]),\,\epsilon(y)\big)
 \ \in\ (\mathbb Q(t)/\Lambda)\rtimes\mathbb Z .
\]
In particular \(\phi_0(y)=(0,\epsilon(y))\) has image \(\mathbb Z\), and is **not** the trivial representation. At \((a,0)\), the two restrictions in the connected-sum cobordism are \(\phi_a\) on \(K\) and this abelian representation on \(-J\). The usual meridian-identifying one-handle/two-handle cobordism extends these representations. Its ordinary intersection form has zero signature (boundary second homology surjects), and its homology over the PTFA quotient skew field is zero, so its twisted signature is zero as well. This is the cobordism calculated in Kim–Kim's proof of Theorem3.1, with the abelian restriction retained. Signature-defect additivity and COT's subgroup property give
\[
 0=\rho(L,\phi_{(a,0)})
   =\rho(K,\phi_a)+\rho_0(-J)
   =\rho(K,\phi_a)-b(V)
   >C-|b(V)|>0,
\]
a contradiction. The choice of \(C\), rather than a false vanishing assertion for the abelian term, completes the argument. This proves the stated fixed-form consequence of Kim's published theorem. We do not here reconstruct its stronger Alexander-polynomial-only conclusion.

For a concrete algebraically concordant family take
\[
 M=\begin{pmatrix}0&2\\1&0\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Its skew part is unimodular, its first coordinate line is a metabolizer, and
\(\det(M-tM^T)=-(2-t)(1-2t)\) is nonunit. For every \(V\), \(M\oplus V\) and \(V\) are algebraically concordant: in \(M\oplus V\oplus(-V)\), take the direct sum of that metabolizer with the diagonal in \(V\oplus(-V)\). It is an isotropic direct summand of half rank. The construction above picks a realization of \(M\oplus V\) that is concordant to no realization of \(V\).

Finally, the literal question asks for forms \(V_1,V_2\) such that **every** pair of their realizations fails concordance. The established statement has quantifiers
\[
 \forall M,V\quad\exists K\text{ realizing }M\oplus V
       \quad\forall J\text{ realizing }V:\ K\not\sim J.
\]
It does not replace the existential quantifier on \(K\) by a universal one. A different realization of \(M\oplus V\) could behave differently. Thus neither category of the prompt is solved by this report. Its contribution is a precise credited obstruction with repaired proof steps and the exact remaining quantifier gap, rather than a claimed new concordance theorem.
