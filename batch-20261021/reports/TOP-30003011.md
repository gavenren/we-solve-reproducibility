# Exact projection-body volume for arbitrary planar-base prisms

For every planar convex body$A$ with nonempty interior and every$h>0$, set$K=A\times[0,h]\subset\mathbb R^3$. Then
\[
R_3(K):=\frac{|\Pi K|}{|K|^2}
 =2\frac{|A-A|}{|A|}\ge8,
\]
with equality exactly when$A$ is centrally symmetric up to translation. Since the Euclidean ball has$R_3(B)=3\pi^2/4<8$, every such prism has strictly larger ratio than an ellipsoid. In particular no prism in this entire subclass is an ellipsoid minimizer. This supplies a dimension-three theorem rather than relying on the earlier unverified claim starting at dimension four.

We prove the projection formula, including its constants. For a planar body, the homogeneous projection support function is its width in the perpendicular direction. Thus$\Pi A=J(A-A)$, where$J$ is a right-angle rotation, and$|\Pi A|=|A-A|$. For a prism, the top and bottom faces together contribute$|A||u_3|$ to the support function of$\Pi K$. The vertical boundary contributes$h h_{\Pi A}(u_1,u_2)$. Consequently
\[
\Pi(A\times[0,h])=(h\Pi A)\times[-|A|,|A|].
\]
For polygonal$A$ this follows immediately from the facet formula
$h_{\Pi K}(u)=\frac12\sum_F |F|\,|u\cdot n_F|$.
For general$A$, approximate by polygons in Hausdorff distance. Projections converge in Hausdorff distance and their areas converge, so their support functions converge uniformly; the formula persists. Taking volumes and dividing by$(h|A|)^2$ proves the identity.

The planar Brunn–Minkowski inequality gives$|A-A|^{1/2}\ge2|A|^{1/2}$. Its equality condition says$A$ and$-A$ are homothetic. Their equal area forces scale one, hence equality means precisely central symmetry after a translation. This proves both the lower bound and its equality characterization without an unverified Petty theorem.

More generally, adjoining an interval to any full-dimensional convex body$C\subset\mathbb R^m$ gives the same orthogonal product formula
\[
\Pi(C\times[0,h])=(h\Pi C)\times[-|C|,|C|],
\qquad R_{m+1}(C\times[0,h])=2R_m(C).
\]
One can verify it first for polytopes by grouping the vertical and horizontal facets and then pass to limits. Therefore a planar body times$d-2$ nondegenerate intervals has
\[
R_d=2^{d-2}|A-A|/|A|\ge2^d.
\]
This exact invariant is independent of the interval lengths and is unchanged under invertible affine maps, so it also covers affine images of these generalized prisms.
