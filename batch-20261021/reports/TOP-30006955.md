# Full P1–P15 on low-rank affine products, with the two-variable tensor step proved

Claim type: **Literature report**.

The fixed2014 list in the prompt holds on every finite direct product of affine Coxeter systems of types$\widetilde G_2$and$\widetilde C_2$, for arbitrary positive integral weight functions on the factors. This is an unbounded-rank class of **infinite, noncommuting**Coxeter groups. The irreducible factor theorems are credited to Guilhot–Parkinson[2,3]. We prove the product passage, including the equality-of-total-$a$issue in the genuine two-variable P15 identity. The earlier Boolean rank-one calculation does not cover that issue.

We use exactly Lusztig[1, §14.2]with$C_w$the triangular bar-invariant basis for$(T_s-v^{L(s)})(T_s+v^{-L(s)})=0$. For$W=W_1\times\cdots\times W_r$, length and weight add, Bruhat order is coordinatewise, and the Hecke algebra is the tensor product over$\mathbb Z[v,v^{-1}]$. Triangularity and bar invariance therefore give
\[
 C_{(w_i)}=\bigotimes_iC_{w_i},\qquad
 h_{x,y,z}=\prod_i h^{(i)}_{x_i,y_i,z_i},\qquad
 p_{1,z}=\prod_i p^{(i)}_{1,z_i}. \tag{1}
\]
The same tensor formula for standard-basis constants proves boundedness: a sum of factor degree bounds is a bound for the product.

Definitions and leading terms now yield
\[
 a(z)=\sum_i a_i(z_i),\quad \Delta(z)=\sum_i\Delta_i(z_i),\quad
 n_z=\prod_i n_{z_i},\quad
 \gamma_{x,y,z}=\prod_i\gamma^{(i)}_{x_i,y_i,z_i}. \tag{2}
\]
The upper bound for$a$is the sum of factor bounds, and the reverse inequality is obtained by independently choosing factor pairs attaining each maximum; their leading coefficients multiply to a nonzero integer. This is not an unproved exchange of suprema. Because factor P1 gives$a_i\le\Delta_i$, equality in their sum holds exactly when equality holds in every coordinate. Thus the distinguished set is$\mathcal D=\prod_i\mathcal D_i$.

The left/right/two-sided preorders, and hence cells, are coordinatewise. Multiplication by a simple generator changes only its factor, and every factor generating relation lifts with the other coordinates fixed, proving both directions. Equations(1)–(2)therefore transfer P1–P8 and P13–P14: inequalities add, nonzero leading triples are nonzero in every factor, signs multiply, and unique distinguished elements are chosen uniquely in each coordinate. For P9–P11, comparability gives a coordinatewise$a$inequality by factor P4. Equality of the total$a$values forces equality in **each**coordinate; the factor equal-$a$cell assertion applies. For P12, every standard parabolic is the product of its factor parabolics, so the parabolic$a$equalities add. This accounts for every property through P14 without supposing that arbitrary equal sums imply coordinate equality.

Here is the remaining P15 step. Write its two sides, with$v'$independent of$v$, as
\[
 L=\sum_z h'_{w,x',z}h_{x,z,y},\qquad
 R=\sum_z h_{x,w,z}h'_{z,x',y},\quad a(w)=a(y). \tag{3}
\]
By(1), each finite sum factors into the product of its coordinate sums$L_i,R_i$. If a summand in$L_i$is nonzero, then$z_i$occurs in$C_{w_i}C_{x'_i}$and$y_i$occurs in$C_{x_i}C_{z_i}$. Hence$y_i\le_{LR}w_i$. The same conclusion holds for a nonzero summand on the right. These occurrence implications follow from the definition of the two-sided based preorder, by expanding multiplication into simple-generator steps. If$y_i\not\le_{LR}w_i$, **both**coordinate sums vanish, so$L=R=0$.

In the remaining case$y_i\le_{LR}w_i$for every$i$, factor P4 gives$a_i(y_i)\ge a_i(w_i)$. Their total sums in(3)are equal, so all these inequalities are equalities. Factor P15 now applies with its actual hypothesis in each coordinate, proving$L_i=R_i$in$\mathbb Z[v^{\pm1},(v')^{\pm1}]$. Multiplication proves(3). No specialization$v'=v$has been used; ordinary associativity at one parameter would not be sufficient.

Guilhot–Parkinson prove all fifteen properties for$\widetilde G_2$with arbitrary allowed positive weights[2]and for$\widetilde C_2$with arbitrary positive weight function[3]. Their work computes actual$a$values and leading data by balanced representations and asymptotic Plancherel theory; it is not verification on a finite ball. Applying the proved product lemma gives the stated family. This report does not reproduce those long factor proofs, nor claim them as new.

Full prior A1 was read: it proves only finite products of rank-one commuting factors, shows an$A_2$braid obstruction, and warns correctly that bounded enumeration does not establish the true$a$function. The present scope adds the published noncommuting affine factors and the total-$a$P15 product argument.
