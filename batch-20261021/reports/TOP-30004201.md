# The exact abelian blue-shift criterion, with the Morava-height indexing spelled out

**Problem:** TOP-30004201

**Claim type:** Literature report

**Exact scope:** For finite abelian p-groups G and H≤G, the compact p-local genuine G-spectrum version has exactly m≥n+rank_p(G/H). Sufficiency also applies to ordinary finite genuine G-spectra by p-localization. No all-nonabelian formula or verification of the earlier announced manuscript is claimed.

Set \(r=\dim_{\mathbb F_p}((G/H)\otimes\mathbb F_p)\), assuming \(G\) is an abelian \(p\)-group. The published result of Barthel–Hausmann–Naumann–Nikolaus–Noel–Stapleton gives the sharp answer, in compact \(p\)-local genuine \(G\)-spectra:
\[
 \boxed{m\geq n+r.}                                               \tag{1}
\]
This includes \(K(0)=H\mathbb Q\). It is the rank of the quotient, not \(\log_p|G/H|\), that occurs.

To verify the indexing, write
\[
 P(H,a)=\{X:K(a-1)_*(\Phi^H X)=0\}.
\]
The literal implication for every \(X\) is exactly
\(P(H,m+1)\subseteq P(G,n+1)\).
Theorem 2.3 of the checked v3 says this inclusion holds if and only if
\[
 H\leq G,\quad G/H\text{ is a }p\text{-group},\quad
 m+1\geq n+1+\operatorname{rank}_p(G/H).
\]
All three conditions are now accounted for; the last is (1). Failure of that inclusion means an actual finite compact \(p\)-local counterexample exists, rather than merely a failure of one proof method.

The source proves an upper bound by the blue-shift behavior of Lubin–Tate spectra, and proves sharpness using equivariant finite partition complexes and their fixed points. Those are substantial imported theorems. The sharpness assertion is not extrapolated from small computed spectra, nor is it inferred from the upper bound alone.

For example, if \(G/H\cong C_{p^s}\) with \(s>1\), then the loss is exactly one, at every finite height. Iterating a cyclic-order-\(p\) theorem through a composition series would only yield loss \(s\), which is a weaker sufficient bound. If \(G/H\cong(C_p)^s\), the sharp loss really is \(s\). These examples distinguish exponent, order and rank without changing the quantifier over spectra.

For an ordinary finite genuine \(G\)-spectrum, its \(p\)-localization is compact \(p\)-locally; geometric fixed points and the Morava \(K\)-theory tests commute with this localization. Thus the sufficient range (1) also gives the original implication there. The necessity statement cited directly here is in the compact \(p\)-local category in which Theorem 2.3 is formulated; no additional descent construction is hidden in that statement.

The checked theorem does not supply a formula for an arbitrary nonabelian \(p\)-group. In particular the prior platform announcement is not used to replace that missing theorem or to infer runtime/formal verification of its claimed proof.
