# Fujita freeness for every polarization on every abelian variety

Claim type: **Partial result**.

Fujita freeness holds for **every ample line bundle on every complex abelian variety**, in all dimensions. In fact if$A$ is an abelian variety and$L$ ample, then
\[
 L^{\otimes m}\text{ is globally generated for every }m\ge2. \tag{1}
\]
Since translation-invariant differentials trivialize$K_A$, (1) proves the exact adjoint$K_A\otimes L^{n+1}$ for$n\ge1$. This includes mixed polarizations on simple abelian varieties; it is not restricted to products of curves or to line bundles already having no base points.

We use the classical theorem of the square: for every line bundle$L$ and points$a,b\in A$,
\[
 t_{a+b}^*L\otimes L\cong t_a^*L\otimes t_b^*L. \tag{2}
\]
Equivalently$a\mapsto t_a^*L\otimes L^{-1}$ is a homomorphism to$\operatorname{Pic}^0(A)$. The exact identity and its proof from the cube theorem are checked in Milne [1, Theorem5.5/Remark5.6]; this classical input is credited. It implies for any$a_1+\cdots+a_m=0$ that
\[
 \bigotimes_{i=1}^m t_{a_i}^*L\cong L^{\otimes m}. \tag{3}
\]
This is an isomorphism of actual line bundles, not merely numerical equivalence, which is essential for global sections.

First$L$ has a nonzero section. On a complex abelian$n$-fold, $K_A$ is trivial, so Kodaira vanishing gives$H^i(A,L)=0$ for$i>0$. Riemann–Roch gives$\chi(L)=c_1(L)^n/n!>0$, since the tangent bundle is trivial and ampleness makes the top intersection positive. Thus$h^0(A,L)>0$. Choose the effective divisor$D$ of such a section. It is a proper closed subset; for$n>0$, it is not the whole variety. The proof below only needs this properness, not smoothness, reducedness or a principal polarization.

Fix a point$x\in A$. For$u\in A$, the section of$t_u^*L$ vanishes at$x$ exactly when$x+u\in D$. Therefore the forbidden set of translations is$D-x$, a proper closed subset of$A$. Choose parameters$a_1,\ldots,a_{m-1}$ freely and set$a_m=-\sum_{i<m}a_i$. For each$i<m$ the condition$x+a_i\in D$ is a proper closed subset of the irreducible parameter variety$A^{m-1}$. The last forbidden condition$x-\sum_{i<m}a_i\in D$ is also proper, because the sum map$A^{m-1}\to A$ is surjective when$m\ge2$. A finite union of proper closed subsets cannot cover this irreducible variety over$\mathbb C$. Hence some parameter tuple avoids all$m$ forbidden conditions.

Multiply the$m$ translated sections. Their tensor product is a section of$\bigotimes_i t_{a_i}^*L$ nonzero at$x$. Transport through(3) to a section of$L^m$ still nonzero at$x$; an isomorphism of one-dimensional fibers cannot turn a nonzero value into zero. As$x$ was arbitrary, this proves(1). The parameters may depend on$x$, exactly as global generation permits. There is no requirement for one section to be nonzero at every point simultaneously.

The coefficient2 in(1) is sharp uniformly: on an elliptic curve a degree-one line bundle is ample and has one section vanishing at its defining point, so$L$ itself need not be generated. Its square is generated by the proof. Thus one cannot replace the argument by “ample means globally generated”. For dimensions$n>1$, Fujita’s exponent$n+1$ is larger than the abelian threshold; our conclusion still concerns the fixed exponent in the prompt, without descending from an unspecified large power.

Earlier A1 proves globally generated polarizations in general and product polarizations on products of curves; neither covers arbitrary ample line bundles on simple higher-dimensional abelian varieties. It also mentions the general dimension-five theorem and newer bounds without auditing their full proofs. Earlier A2 is an unverified all-variety OpenAI claim. The present report supplies a complete classical geometric proof for the whole abelian class. It does not infer the general Fujita assertion or its separate very-ampleness variant.
