# Negative surgery as a broad tight-contact existence class

**Problem:** TOP-2838

**Claim type:** Literature report

**Exact scope:** Precisely attributed 2025 Li–Wan–Zhou preprint: every negative rational surgery on every S³ knot has a positive cooriented tight contact structure with nonzero Heegaard Floer contact invariant. This covers every such surgery manifold that is hyperbolic, without asserting that all oriented hyperbolic manifolds have that surgery description.

A substantial class of the requested oriented hyperbolic manifolds is covered by a recent preprint theorem:
\[
 K\subset S^3,\ r\in\mathbb Q_{>0}
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 S^3_{-r}(K)\text{ has a tight contact structure with }c(\xi)\ne0.
                                                                    \tag{1}
\]
This is Li–Wan–Zhou, Theorem 1.1, in the checked October 2025 v1. In particular, whenever that oriented surgery manifold is hyperbolic, it satisfies the literal existence question. The theorem is reported as a preprint, with its deep Floer-theoretic inputs credited.

The construction is stronger than the elementary observation that negative Legendrian surgery preserves tightness: for an arbitrary knot, a useful Legendrian representative need not lie in the standard tight sphere with the required surgery framing. The source constructs non-loose representatives in potentially overtwisted contact structures and uses their knot Floer invariants.

Specifically Theorem 1.7 supplies a family \(L_{-k}\), \(k\geq0\), with
\[
 tb(L_{-k})=-k,\qquad
 \operatorname{rot}(L_{-k})=-k-2g(K)+1,
\]
nonzero LOSS invariant and compatibility with negative stabilization. These representatives satisfy the source's admissibility condition
\(tb-\operatorname{rot}=2g(K)-1\) and \(tb\ne1\). Theorem 1.6(2) then shows that Legendrian surgery has nonzero contact invariant. Since its smooth framing is \(tb-1=-k-1\), taking \(k=n-1\) yields every negative integer surgery \(-n\). This framing calculation explains the full integer quantifier, rather than only a subsequence of surgeries.

Section 7 passes to negative rational coefficients via the contact rational-surgery construction and preservation of the nonzero contact invariant under the resulting Legendrian surgeries. That step is an imported theorem, not a claim that rational surgery is one smooth two-handle with an integral framing. Nonzero contact invariant implies tightness, although the converse need not hold.

The boundary of this report is both topological and oriented. It has not shown that every closed hyperbolic manifold can be presented as negative rational surgery on a single knot in \(S^3\). Reversing orientation or replacing a knot by its mirror cannot silently erase that issue for positive contact structures: the contact orientation is part of the question. The result therefore gives a large specific existence class with a concrete Floer-theoretic mechanism; it does not prove the universal hyperbolic assertion or require a symplectic filling.

References actually checked:

- Z. Li, S. Wan and H. Zhou, Surgeries on knots and tight contact structures, arXiv:2510.05294v1, 6 October 2025, Theorems 1.1, 1.6, 1.7, Corollary 1.8 and Section 7. https://arxiv.org/pdf/2510.05294
