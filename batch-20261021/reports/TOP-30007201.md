# Torsion-free coefficient injectivity and the Magnusian free-factor strengthening

Claim type: **Literature report**.

For **every torsion-free group** $A$, the coefficient map in the prompt is injective. There is also a stronger free-factor conclusion: let $A=H*B$, let $w\in A*\langle t\rangle$ have exponent sum $\pm1$, and assume $w$ is not conjugate into $H*\langle t\rangle$. In the universal quotient
\[
 Q=(A*\langle t\rangle)/\langle\!\langle w\rangle\!\rangle,
\]
the subgroup generated by the images of $H,t$ is naturally $H*\mathbb Z$. In particular $t$ has infinite order there. This additional conclusion requires the stated support condition; it cannot be attached to every unimodular equation, such as $t=a$.

The source input is Klyachko [1, Theorem1 and Lemma1]. Theorem1 states solvability of any unimodular equation over a torsion-free group in an overgroup containing it. Lemma1 states that such an equation is **Magnusian**: for each free factor $H$ for which the equation is not conjugate to an equation over $H$, some solution in an overgroup is transcendental over $H$. In symbols, for that solution $s$, the natural homomorphism $H*\langle s\rangle\to\widetilde A$ is injective and $\langle s\rangle\cong\mathbb Z$. We credit these difficult theorems and give the universal-quotient deductions carefully.

First suppose the exponent sum is1. A solution is a group inclusion $j:A\hookrightarrow E$ and an $s\in E$ satisfying $w(j(A),s)=1$. The universal property of the free product yields a homomorphism $A*\langle t\rangle\to E$ taking $a\mapsto j(a)$ and $t\mapsto s$. Its kernel contains $w$ and therefore its normal closure. It factors as $Q\to E$. The composite $A\to Q\to E$ is $j$, injective, so $A\to Q$ is injective. This proves the exact map requested in the literal prompt, rather than only existence of some abstract group with a solution.

If $A=H*B$ and the support condition holds, choose the Magnusian solution from Lemma1. The same factorization restricts to
\[
 H*\langle t\rangle\longrightarrow Q\longrightarrow E.
\]
Its composite is injective by transcendence. Therefore its first map is injective, which proves the free-product subgroup assertion **in the universal quotient itself**. The solution supplied for one free factor need not be the same solution supplied for a different free factor; that does not weaken the conclusion, since all the injectivity statements concern this fixed universal quotient. For exponent sum−1, replace the relator by its inverse. It has the same normal closure and the same conjugacy support condition, so the result is unchanged.

This distinction between universal injectivity and a chosen solution also explains why “soluble over $A$” is not taken to mean “soluble inside $A$”. The equation $t^2=a$ over an infinite cyclic $A$ may require a larger group and has exponent2, outside the theorem; even in the unimodular setting a useful Magnusian solution may intentionally introduce a new infinite-order element. No internal solution is promised.

As an explicit unbounded family covered by the stronger assertion, let $A=F(x_1,\ldots,x_r)$, $r\ge2$, and $H=F(x_1,\ldots,x_{r-1})$. For every unimodular word involving the last free factor essentially, meaning not conjugate into $H*\langle t\rangle$, the universal quotient contains a copy of the rank-$r$ free group $H*\langle t\rangle$ as well as an embedded copy of the entire coefficient group $A$. The general statement also allows torsion-free groups with no local indicability assumption. It is not confined to a finite set of free-group examples.

The earlier A1 reports a claimed arbitrary-coefficient theorem and a Lean scope without certifying it. We do not infer that theorem or any formalization from the record. The present result verifies the classical torsion-free boundary using a directly checked primary manuscript, and adds the exact free-factor consequence. The general torsion case of the literal prompt remains outside this report. The long diagrammatic proof of Klyachko's theorem is credited, not restated as an original proof or claimed fully mechanically checked.
