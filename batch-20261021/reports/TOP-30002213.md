# The missing finite-dimensional exception and the exact uniform-Gamma boundary

Claim type: **Literature report**.

The literal statement needs an infinite-dimensional hypothesis: $A=M_r(\mathbb C)$ is unital, simple, separable and nuclear, has nuclear dimension0 and strict comparison, but is not $\mathcal Z$-stable. After excluding finite-dimensional algebras, the two deepest currently checked published results give
\[
 \dim_{\rm nuc}A<\infty\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad A\cong A\otimes\mathcal Z,
 \tag{1}
\]
and, when $T(A)\ne\varnothing$,
\[
 A\cong A\otimes\mathcal Z
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \bigl(A\text{ has strict comparison and uniform property }\Gamma\bigr).
 \tag{2}
\]
Here (1) is Castillejos–Evington–Tikuisis–White–Winter [1, TheoremA], and (2) is Castillejos–Evington–Tikuisis–White [2, Theorem5.6]. These are credited theorems, not a claim to prove the unrestricted comparison implication. The literal counterexample, the corrected infinite-dimensional scope, and the additional uniform hypothesis must be kept separate.

We check the counterexample in full. A finite-dimensional algebra is its own completely positive approximation through one finite-dimensional algebra with one order-zero summand, so nuclear dimension is0. For positive elements in $M_r(\mathbb C)\otimes\mathcal K$, Cuntz subequivalence is determined by the rank of support projections, and the dimension function of the unique normalized trace is rank divided by$r$. Strict inequality of all dimension functions therefore implies the required subequivalence. Thus strict comparison holds. But $M_r(\mathbb C)\otimes\mathcal Z$ contains the infinite-dimensional subalgebra $1\otimes\mathcal Z$ and cannot be isomorphic to $M_r(\mathbb C)$. In particular this is a correction to the exact source wording, not a disproof of the customary non-elementary Toms–Winter conjecture.

The uniform condition in (2) is not merely the existence of a diffuse tracial fiber. For every finite subset $F\subset A$, $\varepsilon>0$ and integer$n\ge1$, there must be pairwise orthogonal positive contractions $e_1,\ldots,e_n\in A$ such that
\[
 \|[e_i,a]\|<\varepsilon,
 \qquad \sup_{\tau\in T(A)}|\tau(ae_i)-\tau(a)/n|<\varepsilon
 \quad(a\in F). \tag{3}
\]
This is the local norm-central characterization in [2, Proposition2.2(iii)]. Equivalently, the uniform tracial ultrapower has central projections summing to1 that split every limit trace equally on every $a\in A$ [2, Definition2.1]. The supremum over the whole trace space and the factor $\tau(a)$ are essential; checking only $\tau(e_i)=1/n$ for individually chosen traces does not prove (3).

Combining (1)–(2) gives the complete three-way equivalence for every infinite-dimensional simple separable unital nuclear algebra with nonempty trace space and property(3). There is also the useful numerical conclusion $\dim_{\rm nuc}A\le1$ when these conditions hold [1, TheoremB]. Nuclear dimension0 is exactly the AF case in this setting [1, CorollaryC], so a non-AF algebra in this class has dimension exactly1. No universal bound is inferred for an algebra assumed only to have strict comparison.

The scope is extensive without being automatic. For example, [2, Proposition5.7] gives uniform$\Gamma$ when the extremal trace boundary is compact and finite-dimensional; hence strict comparison suffices for all algebras in that class. The same source's TheoremA/CorollaryB handles the crossed-product setting with its precise dynamical hypotheses. We do not omit those hypotheses or promote pointwise fiber information to a uniform central sequence. The chain in the proof of [2, Theorem5.6] uses complemented partitions of unity and uniformly tracially large order-zero matrix maps, followed by Matui–Sato comparison machinery; these are substantial inputs credited through that theorem.

Earlier A1 summarizes several broader2026 OpenAI claims, including strict comparison implying $\mathcal Z$-stability in greater generality and other uniform-Gamma assertions. Its underlying manuscripts/formalizations are not verified here. The contribution instead identifies the literal finite-dimensional error and states exactly the verified published implications and the trace-uniform condition bridging them. It neither certifies nor refutes those broader claimed results. We do not assert that the2020 source's open-status remarks alone determine the status of every later unverified claim.
