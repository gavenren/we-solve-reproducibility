# Girth regimes and the twelve-neighbor obstruction for an apex

**Claim type: Literature report.** Aigner-Horev and Krakovski prove that every6-connected graph of girth at least6 contains a \(K_6\) minor, and that the same holds at girth at least5 when
\[
 |E(G)|\ge\tfrac{16}{5}|V(G)|-8
 \tag{1}
\]
[1, results1.2–1.3]. These are credited structural theorems, not reproduced minor arguments. We derive the exact conjecture scopes and a stronger apex-degree restriction than the preceding attempt's order restriction.

A6-connected simple graph has minimum degree at least6. Its girth-six case can therefore never be a \(K_6\)-minor-free counterexample to the prompt: the first source theorem rules it out at **every** order, not just sufficiently large order. At girth five, the same holds under(1). For example minimum degree at least7 gives \(|E|\ge7|V|/2\ge16|V|/5-8\), so all6-connected girth-five graphs of minimum degree at least7 are covered. Minimum degree6 alone gives only \(3|V|\), and should not be substituted for(1) at arbitrary order.

Here is a self-contained apex obstruction. Suppose \(G\) is6-connected and \(G-v=H\) is planar. Write \(m=|V(H)|\) and \(A=N_G(v)\). For \(x\in A\), \(d_H(x)\ge5\); for \(x\notin A\), \(d_H(x)\ge6\). Since \(H\) is a simple planar graph with \(m\ge6\), Euler's bound gives
\[
 \sum_{x\in V(H)}(6-d_H(x))
 =6m-2|E(H)|\ge12.
\]
Each summand at a member of \(A\) is at most1, while every summand outside \(A\) is nonpositive. Consequently
\[
 d_G(v)=|A|\ge12.
 \tag{2}
\]
This concerns the number of **neighbors of the actual apex**, not merely the order of \(H\). In particular a6-connected graph of maximum degree at most11 is not apex.

If equality holds in(2), equality is necessary throughout the curvature calculation. Thus \(H\) is a planar triangulation; every member of \(A\) has degree5 in \(H\), and every other vertex has degree6. Conversely these are the equality conditions for the displayed necessary inequality; they do not by themselves prove6-connectivity after the apex is added.

There is also a useful girth distinction. A6-connected apex graph must contain a triangle **already in \(H\)**. Otherwise a planar triangle-free \(H\) has \(|E(H)|\le2m-4\), contradicting its minimum degree5 and hence \(|E(H)|\ge5m/2\). The planar triangle-free bound follows by counting face boundaries (or by applying it separately to blocks), and \(H\) is in fact5-connected. This explains why the source's girth theorems prove a stronger exclusion than merely producing an apex.

For comparison, Kawarabayashi, Norine, Thomas and Wollan prove that there is an absolute \(N_0\) such that every6-connected \(K_6\)-minor-free graph of order at least \(N_0\) is apex [2, Theorem1.3]. Combining this with(2) shows that every6-connected graph of maximum degree at most11 and order at least \(N_0\) contains a \(K_6\) minor. The statement has an unspecified absolute threshold; this combination supplies neither an effective numerical list nor a proof at all smaller orders.

The preceding attempt already cites the sufficiently-large theorem and derives \(|V(G)|\ge13\) for apex graphs. Our added content is the **all-order girth regimes**, their exact density consequence, and the apex-neighbor curvature bound with equality structure. No claim is made that the large theorem covers its finite exceptional range, or that the maximum-degree obstruction alone supplies a minor certificate. No finite enumeration or program execution is claimed.

[1] Elad Aigner-Horev and Roi Krakovski, *Extremal results regarding \(K_6\)-minors in graphs of girth at least5*, arXiv:1012.5795v1,28December2010, results1.2–1.3: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1012.5795

[2] Ken-ichi Kawarabayashi, Serguei Norine, Robin Thomas and Paul Wollan, *\(K_6\) minors in large6-connected graphs*, arXiv:1203.2192v1,9March2012, Theorem1.3: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1203.2192
