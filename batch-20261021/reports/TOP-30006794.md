# Exact optimality in every dihedral Fourier-label block

Problem: TOP-30006794  
Claim type: Partial result  
Author: /root/hundred_algorithms

**Exact scope.** For N≥1, t≥0 and fixed known Fourier labels k∈(Z/NZ)^t, the optimal uniform-prior success for distinguishing the hidden reflection from t phase-qubit states is (Σx√ηx)²/(N·2^t), where ηx counts subset sums. Every such measurement has success≤min(1,2^t/N). This is a finite-block state-discrimination theorem, not an efficient circuit or a lower bound for arbitrary oracle algorithms.

The optimal pretty-good measurement and its coset-state sample threshold are due to Bacon–Childs–van Dam [1]. The earlier contribution computes an achievable fiber-compression success probability. Here a matching upper bound is proved against every measurement in each fixed-label block. The argument includes composite moduli, empty fibers and degenerate labels; historical novelty for the measurement principle is not claimed.

Write M=2ᵗ, ω=exp(2πi/N), and ηₓ=|{b∈{0,1}ᵗ:k·b=x mod N}|. For every nonempty fiber let |Sₓ⟩ be its normalized uniform superposition. These vectors form an orthonormal basis of a subspace K of dimension r≤min(N,M). A uniform secret a gives
\[
 |\psi_a\rangle=\sum_{\eta_x>0}\sqrt{\eta_x/M}\,\omega^{ax}|S_x\rangle .
\]
Compress any measurement to K; this changes no success probability. A failure outcome can be assigned to an arbitrary guess, so an optimal measurement has N positive effects summing to I_K.

Define Uₐ|Sₓ⟩=ω^(ax)|Sₓ⟩. Averaging any measurement over simultaneous cyclic translations of states and guesses preserves its uniform-prior success. An optimum can therefore be chosen covariant, Eₐ=UₐE₀Uₐ*. Character orthogonality in ΣₐEₐ=I_K forces every diagonal entry of E₀ to equal 1/N. Each positive two-by-two principal minor then implies |(E₀)xy|≤1/N. Consequently
\[
 \langle\psi_0|E_0|\psi_0\rangle
 \le\frac{1}{NM}\left(\sum_x\sqrt{\eta_x}\right)^2 .
\]
The left side is the average success of this covariant measurement. Equality is attained by E₀=(1/N)|u⟩⟨u| with |u⟩=Σx|Sₓ⟩ and its translates: their sum is I_K by the same orthogonality. They may be extended to the entire Hilbert space by adding (I−I_K)/N to each effect. The exact optimum follows.

Cauchy–Schwarz gives (Σ√η)²≤rΣη=rM. Hence success≤r/N≤min(1,M/N). Equal nonempty fiber sizes attain r/N. If all labels vanish, r=1 and success is exactly 1/N regardless of t. If t≤(1−ε)log₂N, the success of every such measurement is at most N^(−ε); constant success requires t≥log₂N−O(1). The N=1 and t=0 boundaries follow from the same formulas.

These effects can have exponentially long descriptions. The proof neither constructs a uniform poly(log N)-size circuit nor prepares subset-sum fiber superpositions efficiently. Furthermore an information lower bound for algorithms given phase/coset states is not automatically a query lower bound for all coherent hiding-oracle strategies. This is why the result does not settle the literal simultaneous time-and-workspace requirement.
